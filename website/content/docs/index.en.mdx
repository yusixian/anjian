**Anjian** is a visual workbench for you and your AI Agent.

Reviewing designs, specs, or prototypes inside a chat box gets messy fast. Anjian renders the **artboards, documents, and HTML pages** (plus whiteboards, slide decks, and storyboards) your Agent creates right in the browser—click the exact spot you want changed, leave a comment, and let your Agent ship the next version.

## Pick your starting point [#start-with-your-situation]

| Your situation | First step |
| --- | --- |
| **You have an account or an invite link** | Click **Open Workbench** to sign in, then pair your local CLI in [Devices & CLI](/en/docs/remote-cli) |
| **You want to try it locally first** | Follow [Local Setup](/en/docs/local) to grab a release package or build from source, then [Create Your First Artifact](/en/docs/first-artifact) |
| **You want whiteboards, slides, storyboards & review docs** | Jump into [Review Workflows](/en/docs/review-workflows) |
| **You want to run your own server** | Follow [Self-Hosting](/en/docs/self-hosting) to deploy with Docker or Dokploy and set up GitHub sign-in |

## What's included [#features]

- **[Artboards](/en/docs/artboards)**: Compare multi-page layouts side by side with pan/zoom, element inspection, pinned comments, option voting, and interactive prototypes.
- **[Documents](/en/docs/documents)**: Rich-text specs with inline comments, direct copy edits in the browser, artifact references, Q&A/approvals, and revision diffs.
- **[HTML Previews](/en/docs/html)**: Run full HTML pages in a sandboxed preview and send selection feedback straight to your Agent.
- **[Whiteboards, Slides & Storyboards](/en/docs/review-workflows)**: Sketch diagrams, present decks with speaker notes, and render storyboard videos to MP4.
- **[Connect an Agent](/en/docs/agent)**: Works with Claude Code, Codex, Pi, Antigravity, Copilot CLI, and custom hosts.
- **[Public Sharing](/en/docs/sharing) & [Inviting Friends](/en/docs/members)**: Share a read-only link with anyone, or invite friends via GitHub to their own workspace.
- **[Library](/en/docs/library) & [Storage](/en/docs/storage)**: Filter by project, bulk manage with a 30-day trash, and connect S3 storage.

## Where your data lives

- **Local mode**: Artifacts live in `.anjian/artifacts/` inside your project folder; server state lives in `~/.anjian/server/`.
- **Self-hosted mode**: Everything stays on your own server and syncs explicitly via CLI `push` and `pull`.

Anjian has no cloud account, no subscription, and no upstream auto-updater. See [License & Credits](/en/docs/about) or check out a [real design-feedback walkthrough](/en/use-cases).

#### 中文

可以从本机私有文档页头打开「验收检查」，重跑在 Hub 上明确登记的项目命令。结果面板可展开查看输出和 SHA-256，运行历史与原报告中的证据分别保留。

<!-- anjian:en -->

#### English

Open **Verification checks** in a private document on your local Hub to rerun a registered project command. Expand a result to see the output and SHA-256. Run history is kept alongside the evidence already recorded in the report.

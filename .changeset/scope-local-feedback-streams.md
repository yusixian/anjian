#### 中文

本机 Hub 按每个监听连接订阅的项目和作品发送批注通知。移除一件作品后，连接继续接收其他作品的反馈，重连时只恢复仍有效的订阅。

<!-- anjian:en -->

#### English

The local Hub sends comment notifications only to the projects and artifacts subscribed to by each listener. Removing one artifact keeps the other subscriptions active. Reconnecting restores only the subscriptions still in effect.

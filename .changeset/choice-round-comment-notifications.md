#### 中文

在画板开放的选案轮次里，对板块或分组添加批注，已订阅该画板的本地 Agent 会话会立即收到通知。点选选项保持安静，提交轮次时会收到一条选案摘要。

<!-- anjian:en -->

#### English

Comment on a board or group in an open choice round to notify a local agent session subscribed to that artboard immediately. Picking an option stays quiet, and submitting the round sends one choice summary.

#### 中文

分镜素材库新增「旁白」。添加一行，填写原文和语音语言。可用发音词表指定专有名词读音，或用单行读音覆盖整行。使用本机 macOS 案间和 FFmpeg 时，可以生成试听，再把满意的音频加入音轨。脚本随分镜保存，录音和音频导入仍可使用。

<!-- anjian:en -->

#### English

Open Narration in the storyboard media bin. Add a line with its original text and voice language. Use the pronunciation glossary for names and terms, or enter a spoken line to override it. With local Anjian on macOS and FFmpeg, generate a preview and add it to an audio track. The script saves with the storyboard, and you can keep importing your own audio.

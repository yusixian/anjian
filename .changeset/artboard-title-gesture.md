#### 中文

缩放画板时，标题会尽量保持手势开始时的屏幕字号，手势结束后再调整。空间变窄可能遮住板块时，字号会随手势缩小以腾出空间。

<!-- anjian:en -->

#### English

While you zoom an artboard, titles keep their starting screen size and update when the gesture ends. A title can shrink during the gesture if it needs room beside a board.

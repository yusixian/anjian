#### 中文

用 Tab 聚焦分镜监看器的播放区域。空格播放或暂停，左右方向键后退或前进五秒。素材预览中的跳转会停在当前片段边界。

<!-- anjian:en -->

#### English

Tab to the storyboard monitor's playback area. Press Space to play or pause, or use the arrow keys to seek backward or forward five seconds. Source preview seeks stay within the selected clip.

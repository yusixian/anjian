#### 中文

保存幻灯片后，其他打开同一私有作品的编辑、观众和演讲者窗口会自动更新。Agent 从源文件增删或重排页面也会同步。编辑窗口保留未保存的草稿，并提示另一个人或 Agent 已更新卡组。

<!-- anjian:en -->

#### English

Saving a slide deck refreshes its other open private editor, audience, and presenter windows. Slides added, removed, or reordered by an Agent in the source file also sync. Editors keep unsaved drafts and report a revision conflict when someone else updates the deck.

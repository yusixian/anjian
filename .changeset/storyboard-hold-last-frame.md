#### 中文

分镜导出时，可把视频片段设置得比素材剩余时长更长。素材播完后，画面停在最后一帧直到片段结束，原音轨按实际时长结束。

<!-- anjian:en -->

#### English

When exporting a storyboard, a video clip can extend past the remaining source duration. The picture holds its final frame until the clip ends, and the source audio ends at its original boundary.

#### 中文

分镜监看器中，用鼠标点击视频画面可以播放或暂停，并把焦点移到监看器。点击后可按空格播放或暂停，或按左、右方向键后退或前进五秒。

<!-- anjian:en -->

#### English

Click the video picture in the storyboard monitor with a mouse to play or pause and focus the monitor. Then use Space to play or pause or the Left and Right arrow keys to seek by five seconds.

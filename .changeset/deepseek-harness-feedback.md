#### 中文

DeepSeek Harness 会话可以用 `/anjian watch document/plan` 订阅本机案间批注。通知进入对应会话，读取批注后才推进该会话的回执。

<!-- anjian:en -->

#### English

Use `/anjian watch document/plan` in DeepSeek Harness to receive local Anjian comment notifications. Comments stay unread for that session until it reads them.

#### 中文

分镜监看器同时显示播放位置和总时长。支持视频帧回调的浏览器会按实际显示的画面更新位置。视频缓冲时，时间码停留在最近显示的画面。

<!-- anjian:en -->

#### English

The storyboard monitor shows the playback position and total duration. In browsers with video frame callbacks, the position follows the frame on screen and holds at that frame while the video buffers.

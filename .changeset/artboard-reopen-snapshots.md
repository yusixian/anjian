#### 中文

重开画板时，远处的板块可以复用本浏览器上次保存的截图。缩放或平移到板块附近会加载实时内容。作品文件更新后，重开会使用当前内容。可交互板块直接加载，也可以在浏览器禁用存储时照常打开画板。

<!-- anjian:en -->

#### English

When you reopen an artboard, distant boards can reuse snapshots saved by the same browser. Zoom or pan closer to load the live board. Reopening after artifact files change uses the current content. Interactive boards open live, and artboards still open when browser storage is disabled.
